The equalizer that lives in your menu bar.

EQBar is a native Mac app with a system-wide equalizer, AutoEQ headphone correction, spatial audio, per-app volume and Audio Unit plugins. It idles at about 10 MB and needs no audio driver.

Spatial audio

Speakers in a room, on your headphones

EQBar places your stereo on two virtual speakers in front of you, inside a simulated room, using Apple's AUSpatialMixer.

  • Headphones get Apple's high-quality HRTF renderer
  • Speakers use equal-power panning
  • 13 rooms, from a small room to a cathedral
  • Crossfeed and stereo width on the same page
  • Detects headphones on its own, with an optional personalized profile

How it works

Built on Core Audio process taps

macOS 14.4 added process taps, a public API for capturing what apps play. EQBar taps the system mix, mutes the original, runs it through its signal chain and plays it on your output. Nothing to install in the system.

  1. Your apps play as usual
  2. ↓
  3. Process tapcaptures the mix, mutes the original
  4. ↓
  5. Preampheadroom for boosts
  6. Pluginsyour Audio Units
  7. Equalizerup to 64 bands
  8. SpatialHRTF and rooms
  9. Crossfeedbs2b
  10. Stereo widthmid/side
  11. Volumeboost to +12 dB
  12. Limiterstops clipping
  13. ↓
  14. Your outputheadphones, speakers, HDMI
Real-time safeThe audio thread never allocates memory or waits on a lock.
About 5 to 11 ms addedOne I/O buffer of latency between the tap and your output.
Silence costs nothingWhen nothing plays, EQBar skips the signal chain entirely.

Privacy

Two permissions, both explained

Your audio is processed on your Mac. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and there's no account, analytics or telemetry.

Required

System Audio Recording

Lets EQBar tap the sound your Mac plays so it can process it. macOS asks on first launch.

Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording
Optional

Accessibility

Only used to see the volume and mute keys. EQBar ignores every other key. Turn volume keys off and it isn't needed.

Privacy & Security › Accessibility
On demand

Network

Install

Up and running in a minute

A universal app for macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. Download the release, or build it yourself with only the Command Line Tools.

  1. Download EQBar-1.0.0.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Move EQBar.app to your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. The app isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks once:
    • macOS 15 and later: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway
    • macOS 14: Control-click the app, choose Open, then Open again
    • Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/EQBar.app
  4. Allow System Audio Recording, and Accessibility if you want the volume keys.
